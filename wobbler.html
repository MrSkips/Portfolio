Wobbler Engine

A completed manufacturing project connecting casting, machining, and assembly.

PROJECT

Manufacturing lab

STATUS

Completed · 2026

METHODS

Manual milling · Green-sand casting · Assembly

From drawing to physical part

I interpreted an ASME Y14.5 drawing and machined a 6061 aluminum cylinder plate on a manual vertical mill. I established work zero, located features with an edge finder and DRO, then faced, drilled, reamed, and hand tapped 1/4-20 UNC and 1/4-28 UNF features.

Casting and assembly

I prepared a two-part green-sand mold with sprues, a runner, and a pouring basin. The completed project brought together casting, machining, and final engine assembly.
