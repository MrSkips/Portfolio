TEAM PROJECT · 2025

ThermaVest Heated Sleeve

Enclosure design, prototype fabrication, and thermal measurements for a heated sleeve.

MY ROLE

CAD lead · Four-person team

WHEN

January–April 2025

TOOLS

SolidWorks · FDM · FLIR · Multimeter

Designing around the components

I designed a SolidWorks enclosure around a 7.4 V, 2,200 mAh Li-ion battery pack, switch, and wiring harness. I added vents to promote heat dissipation and supported FDM fabrication and prototype assembly.

Measuring the prototype

Our team used FLIR imaging and a multimeter to record approximately 96°F at the sleeve, a 4.3°F battery temperature rise over ten minutes, and a 2.2 A initial current draw.

Battery capacity and initial draw suggested about one hour of runtime. This was an estimate, rather than a full discharge test.
