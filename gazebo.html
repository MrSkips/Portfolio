PERSONAL PROJECT · JULY 2025

Gazebo Frame Repair

A measured replacement for a failed threaded insert, refined through three printed iterations.

MEASURE→MODEL→PRINT→CHECK
PROJECT

Personal repair

WHEN

July 2025

TOOLS

Calipers · SolidWorks · Bambu Lab P1S

The problem

A threaded insert in a gazebo frame had failed. The replacement needed to retain both the threaded connection and the press fit without changing the surrounding assembly.

Three rounds of iteration

I measured the failed insert with calipers and modeled a parametric replacement in SolidWorks. Across three FDM prints, I adjusted wall thickness and infill, checking each version against the physical interface.

The result

The final PLA replacement, printed on a Bambu Lab P1S, installed in the existing frame without modifying adjacent parts.
